PAWSOME PADDOCK bookings

Welcome to Northumberland Canine Centre PAWSOME PADDOCK booking system. Hopefully you will find the process easy.

  • You will first need to register your details. This is a one-off registration.
  • Book your required time slot(s). This can be changed or deleted if you make a mistake, or you wish to cancel.
  • Your booking will be emailed to us immediately 
  • The code for the Paddock gate will be emailed to you
  • Please note; as we are a training centre and the paddock is regularly used for classes, we cannot accept bitches in season.

Bookings charges are as follows;    1- 2 dogs  1 hour...........£12   30 mins.........£7                                       Additional dogs...... £2 per dog per booking  

Booking times include change over time. Please respect the next customer coming in.

PAYMENT     Bank Transfer; Northumberland Canine Centre

            30-90-13   00440804   Ref; SURNAME + PADDOCK

 

                 For cash payment please hand in to the arena when possible. If there is no one there, please place your payment into the mailbox inside the Paddock. Payment should be put in an envelope with details of your name and booking time clearly marked on the outside. NO loose money.

Failure to attend will still incur the cost unless booking is cancelled 24hrs before.

If you are having problems booking, please contact Jacquie on 07813 912889
